Pill-shaped call to action — "Book Now", "Explore Services", "See Pricing".

```jsx
<Button>Book Now</Button>
<Button variant="outline" size="sm">See Pricing</Button>
```

- `primary` (violet) is the default CTA on white; `inverse` or `accent` on violet grounds.
- `ghost` is an underlined text link with a gold underline.
- Labels are uppercase and short (1–3 words).
