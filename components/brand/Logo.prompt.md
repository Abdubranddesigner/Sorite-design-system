Soreti logo lockup or lotus symbol, from the official artwork.

```jsx
<Logo width={180} assetBase="../assets/logo" />
<Logo variant="white" />          {/* on Royal Violet */}
<Logo variant="lotus" width={56} />
```

- Keep clear space equal to the height of the "S" around the lockup.
- Set `assetBase` to wherever you copied `assets/logo/`.
