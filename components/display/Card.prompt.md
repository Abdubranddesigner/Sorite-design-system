Container for a service category, package, location or testimonial.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Hammam Journeys" title="Royal Moroccan Hammam" footer={<Button variant="ghost">See pricing</Button>}>
  Beldi black soap, Kessa exfoliation and Rassoul clay, finished with an argan oil massage.
</Card>
```

- `violet` for featured/signature items; `tint` for quiet asides.
