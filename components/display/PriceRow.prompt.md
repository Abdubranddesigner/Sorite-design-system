```jsx
<PriceRow name="Ethiopian Coffee Energizing"
  description="Coffee oil stimulates circulation, firms skin, and fights fatigue."
  prices={[{ label: '60 MIN', amount: '3,400' }, { label: '90 MIN', amount: '3,900' }]} />
```

- Prices in ETB with thousands comma; durations uppercase "MIN".
